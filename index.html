How it works

Type. Tap. Send.

Nothing new to learn. KEYBO works wherever you already type.

  1. 1

    Type, or copy

    Write in any app as usual. To answer someone, copy their message.

  2. 2

    Tap a KEYBO button

    Reply, a tone, Fix, Rewrite or Translate, right above your keys.

  3. 3

    Pick it and send

    Choose the version you like. Not quite right? Undo and try another.

Set up in a minute

Two steps, and the app walks you through both.

Step 1

Allow KEYBO

  1. Open Settings from the KEYBO app
  2. Tap Keyboards, turn on KEYBO
  3. Turn on Allow Full Access, tap Allow
Step 2

Choose KEYBO

  1. Tap any text box
  2. Touch and hold the 🌐 globe key
  3. Slide to KEYBO and let go

Full Access is only for the AI buttons. Typing works without it.

Step 1

Turn it on

  1. Open KEYBO, tap Open Settings
  2. Switch Keybo on in the on-screen keyboard list
  3. Tap OK on the notice Android shows for every keyboard
Step 2

Make it your keyboard

  1. Back in the app, tap Choose Keybo
  2. Pick Keybo in the Choose input method box
  3. Type something and tap Fix

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is KEYBO free?

Yes. The keyboard is free, with 10 AI actions every day. Premium removes the daily limit and adds sticky tones and long replies.

Does KEYBO read everything I type?

No. Typing, suggestions and autocorrect happen on your phone. Text leaves your phone only when you tap an AI button, over an encrypted connection. It isn't stored after the answer comes back, and it's never sold. Password fields are never read.

Why does iPhone ask for "Full Access"?

Apple requires Full Access for any keyboard that uses the internet, and the AI buttons need it to write your answer. Without it, KEYBO still works as a full keyboard; only the AI buttons are greyed out.

How does Reply work on iPhone?

Copy the message you received, tap Reply on KEYBO, and pick an answer. iPhone doesn't let keyboards read other apps' screens, so copying is how KEYBO knows what to answer.

If iPhone keeps asking "Allow Paste", go to Settings › Apps › KEYBO › Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.

Can I make my own tone?

Yes. In KEYBO's settings, open Custom tones, give it a name and describe how it should sound. It appears on your keyboard next to the built-in tones.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, KEYBO is one app for iPhone and iPad.

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › KEYBO. On Android: Play Store › Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Email hellokeybo@gmail.com, or see the support page.
