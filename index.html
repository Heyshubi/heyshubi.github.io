Private by design

Your words are yours. Full stop.

A keyboard sees everything you type, so we built KEYBO to look away. Here's exactly what happens to your text.

Sent only when you tap

Text goes to our server only when you tap an AI button, over an encrypted connection. Never while you type.

Not stored, never sold

Your text is used to write the answer and isn't kept after the answer comes back. No keystroke logging.

Passwords stay sealed

Nothing you type in a password field is ever read or sent. Not once.

Learning stays on your phone

Words the keyboard learns stay on your device, and you can erase them in Settings anytime.

The keys work fully without Full Access; only the AI buttons need it. Read the full privacy policy.

Setup

Ready in under a minute.

The app walks you through it step by step. Here's what it looks like.

Step 1 of 2

Allow KEYBO

  1. Open Settings (the KEYBO app has a button for it)
  2. Tap Keyboards
  3. Turn on KEYBO
  4. Turn on Allow Full Access, then tap Allow
  5. Come back to KEYBO

Full Access is only for the AI buttons, which need the internet. Typing works without it.

Step 2 of 2

Choose KEYBO

  1. Tap any text box so a keyboard opens
  2. Touch and hold the 🌐 globe key, bottom left
  3. Slide to KEYBO and let go

That's it. KEYBO stays your keyboard until you switch back with the same globe key.

Step 1 of 2

Turn it on

  1. Open the KEYBO app and tap Open keyboard settings
  2. Find KEYBO in the list and switch it on
  3. Android shows a standard warning that keyboards can read what you type. It appears for every keyboard; tap OK
Step 2 of 2

Make it your keyboard

  1. Back in the app, tap Choose KEYBO
  2. Pick KEYBO from the list your phone shows
  3. Type something and tap Fix above the keys to try it

You can switch back anytime from the keyboard icon in your navigation bar.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is KEYBO free?

Yes. The whole keyboard is free, with 10 AI actions every day. Premium removes the daily limit and adds sticky tones and long replies.

Does KEYBO read everything I type?

No. Typing, suggestions and autocorrect all happen on your phone. Your text leaves your phone only when you tap an AI button, and only the text that button needs. It travels over an encrypted connection, isn't stored after the answer comes back, and is never sold.

Anything typed in a password field is never read or sent.

Why does iPhone ask me to allow "Full Access"?

Apple requires Full Access for any keyboard that connects to the internet, and the AI buttons need the internet to write your reply. Apple shows the same standard warning for every keyboard that asks for it.

Without Full Access, KEYBO still works as a full keyboard; only the AI buttons are greyed out.

How does Reply work on iPhone?

Copy the message you received, open the reply box, and tap Reply on KEYBO. You'll get short, medium and long answers to choose from. iPhone doesn't let keyboards read other apps' screens, so copying the message is how KEYBO knows what to answer.

If iPhone keeps asking "Allow Paste", go to Settings › Apps › KEYBO › Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.

Can I type by voice?

On iPhone and iPad, Apple doesn't let keyboards use the microphone. Instead, share a voice note to KEYBO and it's turned into text on your device; the audio never leaves your phone.

Which languages does KEYBO support?

Translate works in 35 languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Spanish, French, Arabic and Japanese. You can also type Hindi in Roman letters (Hinglish) and get Devanagari, or rewrite any message in natural Hinglish.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. KEYBO is one app for iPhone and iPad, and on iPad the keyboard is laid out big and clear for the larger screen.

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › KEYBO. On Android: Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews and you won't be charged again.

How do I get in touch?

Write to hellokeybo@gmail.com. We read every message. There's more help on the support page.
